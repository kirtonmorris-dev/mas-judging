You already know where this goes wrong.

Every committee runs into the same three moments, whether it's a Panorama semifinal or a kiddies costume competition.

Paper sheets get lost or smudged.

Scores travel by hand between judges and a tabulation table, and there's no record of what happened in between.

Totals get added by hand, under pressure.

A rushed manual tally, with a crowd waiting, is exactly where a legitimate placement gets challenged later.

Results wait on whoever has the calculator.

The band that just performed, and everyone in the stands, ends up waiting far longer than the performance itself.

How It Works

Three steps, from PIN entry to a printed result.

1

Judges score on their phone.

They log in with a PIN, no app to install, and score each contestant against your own criteria and point scale.

2

Results tally live.

Organizers watch the tally update the moment each judge submits, with any drop-high, drop-low, or averaging rule applied automatically.

3

Print the official sign-off sheet.

Close out the category and print a signed record for the podium, with no transcription step in between.

Why It's Different

Built for a pan yard, not a boardroom.

Generic judging software is built for hackathons and pageants. Judge D Show is built for the specific rules and conditions of Carnival competition.

No score is ever lost.

Load-tested at 20 simultaneous judges scoring at once, with zero errors and no overwritten submissions.
